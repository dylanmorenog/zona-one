# Zona One — Cyber Café Management System

A full-stack web application built to manage the daily operations 
of a small cyber café business in Sanchez Magallanes, Mexico.

## Problem

Small cyber café businesses often rely on manual records to track 
daily income, financial transactions, and operations — leading to 
errors, theft risk, and lack of visibility into business performance.

## Solution

Zona One centralizes daily operations into a single platform, 
replacing manual processes with a structured, auditable system.

## Features

- **Daily income tracking** — Register and monitor revenue by day
- **Financial transactions** — Log cash withdrawals and deposits 
  handled on behalf of customers
- **Report generation** — View summaries of business performance 
  over time

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js + TypeScript
- **ORM:** Prisma
- **Database:** PostgreSQL

## Motivation

Built out of a real need — I manage this business and needed a 
tool that didn't exist yet.
